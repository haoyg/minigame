Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pokémon's 30th anniversary?

It marks 30 years since Pokémon Red and Pokémon Green were released in Japan on February 27, 1996. The official Pokémon anniversary site identifies February 27, 2026 as the 30th anniversary.

What Pokémon products are releasing in December 2026?

Official sources support a December 18 release for three Pokémon visual art books, an early-December window for a Moncolle Paldea Legendary Set, and a December month window for Pokémon Kids anniversary products. The Binder Collection's December 4 date remains unconfirmed here because its official product page currently shows a different launch month.

When will the Pokémon 30th anniversary art books be released?

Overlap lists all three volumes for simultaneous release in Japan on December 18, 2026.

How much do the anniversary art books cost?

The official publisher lists each volume at ¥5,500 including tax. They are sold as three separate books, not as one ¥5,500 set.

Are the books available in English?

No English-language edition has been announced on the official publisher page checked for this guide. The confirmed editions are Japanese publications.

Where can international fans buy them?

No worldwide official purchase route is confirmed. International fans should check the publisher's linked retailers and verify whether a retailer ships to their country; Japanese availability should not be assumed to include international delivery.

Are the products limited edition?

The official pages reviewed here do not consistently label every listed product as limited edition. Availability may still be constrained, but this guide does not claim limited status without a product-specific official statement.

Will more anniversary products be announced?

More announcements are possible, but no complete future schedule is guaranteed. This page will add products only after an official product page or manufacturer announcement can be verified.
