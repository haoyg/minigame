Travian is a classic browser strategy game about villages, tribes, resources, and long-term warfare. Build economy, train troops, and plan expansion on a persistent map.

How to Play Travian

Click or tap buildings, resource fields, troops, reports, maps, and village actions. The main task is to develop villages and compete on a strategy map. Your goal is to grow resources, troops, and territory efficiently. The main challenges are resource limits, attack timing, rival players, and expansion pressure. Aim to build durable villages and compete successfully over time.

Game Controls

  • Keyboard: Mouse or touch controls; keyboard is not required
  • Mouse or touch: Click to build, upgrade fields, train units, send attacks, trade, and review reports
  • Mobile: Tap, swipe, or drag depending on the game.

Tips and Tricks

  • Upgrade resource fields consistently in the early game.
  • Use reports to learn from each attack or raid.
  • Do not leave resources unprotected for long periods.
  • Plan settlers and expansion before resources cap out.
